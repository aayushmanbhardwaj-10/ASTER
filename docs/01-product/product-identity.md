# Product Identity

**What is ASTER?**  
ASTER is an AI‑powered financial intelligence platform that serves as an intelligent workspace for professionals. It combines deep financial reasoning, secure data handling, and intuitive AI‑driven tools—such as conversational agents, automated analysis, visualization, and workflow automation—to help users think, learn, create, analyze, organize, automate, and decide with confidence.

**What is ASTER NOT?**  
ASTER is not a generic chatbot or a generic productivity suite. It does not aim to be a one‑size‑fits‑all AI assistant for casual conversation, nor does it replace specialized trading execution systems, core banking platforms, or enterprise resource planning (ERP) software. Instead, it focuses exclusively on enhancing financial analysis, modeling, and decision‑support workflows with trusted, domain‑specific AI capabilities.

**Who should use ASTER?**  
ASTER is designed for finance and business professionals who require reliable, high‑stakes AI assistance—including financial analysts, investment bankers, portfolio managers, CFOs, controllers, auditors, corporate strategists, entrepreneurs, venture capitalists, researchers, and consultants. Anyone whose workflow involves financial data interpretation, valuation, risk assessment, scenario planning, or business decision‑making will find ASTER purpose‑built to augment their expertise and improve outcomes.
