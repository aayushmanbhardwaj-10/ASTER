# User Personas

## User Persona: Maya Sharma – Senior Financial Analyst

**Demographics**  
- Age: 32  
- Gender: Female  
- Location: New York City, NY (works for a mid‑size investment bank)  
- Education: MBA in Finance, CFA Level II candidate  
- Experience: 5 years in equity research and valuation modeling  

**Professional Context**  
Maya works on the equity research team, building detailed financial models, performing valuation analyses, and preparing investment recommendations for coverage of mid‑cap technology companies. Her day involves pulling data from multiple sources (Bloomberg, FactSet, internal databases), cleaning and normalizing financial statements, running scenario analyses, and creating presentation decks for portfolio managers and sales teams. She frequently collaborates with associates, associates‑in‑training, and the compliance team to ensure models adhere to firm standards.

**Goals & Motivations**  
- Produce accurate, insight‑driven valuation models quickly to meet tight reporting deadlines.  
- Reduce repetitive manual data‑gathering and formula‑writing tasks so she can focus on higher‑level analysis and storytelling.  
- Maintain model transparency and auditability to satisfy internal reviews and external regulators.  
- Stay current with emerging financial analysis techniques and AI‑assisted tools that can give her an edge in generating alpha.  

**Pain Points & Frustrations**  
- Spending excessive time (up to 30 % of her day) on data wrangling—copy‑pasting financial statements, aligning fiscal periods, and normalizing non‑GAAP items.  
- Difficulty ensuring model consistency across team members; small formula errors can propagate and cause rework.  
- Limited insight from raw numbers; she wishes she could quickly run “what‑if” scenarios and see visual impact on key metrics like IRR or EV/EBITDA without rebuilding the model each time.  
- Concerns about data security when using external AI tools; she needs assurance that sensitive company financials never leave the firm’s environment.  

**How ASTER Helps Maya**  
- **AI‑Powered Data Ingestion:** ASTER’s secure connectors pull financial statements directly from Bloomberg/FactSet and auto‑normalize them, cutting data‑prep time by half.  
- **Smart Modeling Assistant:** By describing a valuation approach in natural language (e.g., “build a three‑stage DCF with terminal growth of 2.5 %”), ASTER generates a starter model with correct formulas, links, and documentation, which Maya can then refine.  
- **Scenario & Sensitivity Engine:** One‑click scenario toggles let Maya test multiple assumptions (e.g., revenue growth, WACC) and instantly see tornado charts and sensitivity tables, enabling rapid insight generation.  
- **Explainable AI & Audit Trail:** Every AI‑generated cell includes a traceable comment showing the source data and logic, satisfying compliance reviewers and reducing audit fatigue.  
- **Secure, On‑Prem/Private Cloud Option:** ASTER can be deployed within the firm’s VPC, ensuring that all financial data remains behind the firewall, meeting the firm’s strict data‑governance policies.  
- **Collaboration Hub:** Models can be shared via a web‑based workspace where teammates comment directly on cells, track changes, and approve versions without leaving the platform.  

**Technical Proficiency**  
- Comfortable with Excel, VBA, Python (pandas, NumPy), and basic SQL.  
- Familiar with BI tools (Tableau, Power BI) for visualization.  
- Open to learning new AI‑assisted interfaces if they demonstrably save time and improve accuracy.  

**Success Metrics for Maya**  
- Reduction in average time to produce a full valuation model from 8 hours to ≤4 hours.  
- Increase in the number of actionable insights generated per model (measured by unique scenario insights flagged by her team).  
- Zero critical audit findings related to model errors or data handling in quarterly reviews.  
- Higher satisfaction scores in internal surveys measuring “analytical confidence” and “tool usability.”
