# Target Users

ASTER is designed for professionals who require reliable, high-stakes AI assistance in financial and business decision-making contexts. This includes:

- Financial analysts, investment bankers, portfolio managers, and wealth management professionals
- Corporate finance teams (CFOs, controllers, treasurers, financial planners)
- Auditors, risk managers, and compliance officers
- Corporate strategists, business development professionals, and management consultants
- Entrepreneurs, venture capitalists, and private equity professionals
- Researchers and academics in finance, economics, and business fields
- Technology professionals working on financial systems, fintech solutions, or quantitative analysis
- Anyone whose workflow involves financial data interpretation, valuation, risk assessment, scenario planning, or strategic business decisions requiring trusted AI augmentation.
