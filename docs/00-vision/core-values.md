# Core Values

1. **Trustworthiness**  
   We build AI that professionals can rely on for high‑stakes financial decisions. This means rigorous accuracy, transparent reasoning, robust security, and strict adherence to financial regulations. Trust is earned through consistent performance, verifiable outputs, and a commitment to protecting user data and privacy.

2. **Excellence in Craft**  
   Every line of code, every model, and every user interaction is crafted with meticulous attention to quality. We pursue technical excellence, continuous learning, and relentless improvement, ensuring our platform is reliable, performant, and elegant.

3. **Customer Obsession**  
   Our users—financial analysts, bankers, CFOs, entrepreneurs, and researchers—are at the heart of everything we do. We listen deeply to their workflows, pain points, and aspirations, and we iteratively shape ASTER to solve real‑world problems and amplify their impact.

4. **Collaborative Integrity**  
   We foster a cross‑functional, respectful environment where diverse expertise—engineering, AI, finance, security, design, and product—comes together. Open communication, honest feedback, and shared ownership drive better outcomes and uphold our ethical standards.

5. **Innovation with Purpose**  
   We push the boundaries of what AI can do for finance, but always with a clear purpose: to empower better decisions, reduce risk, and create economic value. Experimentation is guided by measurable impact, not novelty for its own sake.

6. **Resilient Integrity**  
   We operate with transparency, accountability, and a long‑term perspective. When faced with trade‑offs, we choose the path that preserves our credibility and the trust of our users, even if it requires short‑term sacrifice.

7. **Inclusive Empowerment**  
   We believe sophisticated financial intelligence should be accessible to teams of all sizes and backgrounds. By lowering barriers to advanced analysis, we help level the playing field and foster broader economic participation.
